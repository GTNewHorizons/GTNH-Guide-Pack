# Ethereal Familiar Recipe Consolidation
The recipes for Gadomancy's <ItemLink id="gadomancy:ItemEtherealFamiliar:0:{aspect:terra}"/> <ItemImage id="gadomancy:ItemEtherealFamiliar:0:{aspect:terra}"/> have been consolidated in NEI. Each unique recipe is shown <Color id="BLUE">once</Color> rather than being shown for every aspect, removing <Color id="BLUE">1292</Color> (yes, that is more recipes than <Color id="RED">__everything else__</Color> that uses infusion) recipes from the infusion list.

Recipes for terra-aspected familiars are shown by default.
